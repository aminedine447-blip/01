Preamble

We, the students, teachers and staff of Mohamed Lamine Debaghine Secondary School, believe that education is the key to building a better future. Our ideal school is a place of knowledge, respect, creativity and equal opportunities. This charter establishes the rights, duties and rules that everyone must respect to create a safe and successful learning environment.

Article 1

Our School's Values

Our school is based on five fundamental values.

01

Respect and Equality

02

Responsibility and Honesty

03

Cooperation and Teamwork

04

Creativity and Innovation

05

Discipline and Hard Work

Article 2

Students' Rights

Every student has the right to:

  • Receive a high-quality education.
  • Be treated fairly and respectfully.
  • Express their opinions freely and peacefully.
  • Study in a clean, safe and comfortable environment.
  • Use the school's facilities and participate in extracurricular activities.
Article 3

School Facilities

Our ideal school should provide modern, clean and accessible facilities that help every student learn and develop.

  • Well-equipped science and computer laboratories.
  • A large and clean playground for sports and activities.
  • Comfortable classrooms with suitable learning equipment.
  • A school cafeteria that offers healthy meals.
  • Clean toilets and drinking water for everyone.
  • Accessible spaces for students with disabilities.
Article 4

School Libraries

Every student should have access to a welcoming library that encourages reading, research and independent learning.

  • Provide a wide collection of books in different subjects and languages.
  • Offer a quiet and comfortable place for reading and studying.
  • Give students access to computers and useful learning resources.
  • Organize reading clubs and cultural activities.
  • Keep the library organized and make books available to all students.
